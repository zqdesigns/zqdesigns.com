Privacy policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

This policy covers ZQDesigns.com and Lesson Draft Staging, a private tool that connects my content workstation to a Google account. The short version: the website collects nothing, and the app only touches files it creates in the one Google account I set up for it, only to do the job described here.

Who runs this

Joshua Bishop runs ZQDesigns.com and Lesson Draft Staging as part of his work at SwellFox, Inc. in Boise, Idaho. In this policy, “I” and “me” mean Joshua Bishop. You can reach me through LinkedIn.

The website

ZQDesigns.com has no cookies, analytics, ads, forms, or tracking scripts, and its fonts are served from the site itself. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses to keep the site and its service secure. GitHub handles that data under its privacy statement.

Who Lesson Draft Staging is for

Lesson Draft Staging isn't a public service. There's no sign-up, and it isn't offered to anyone else. The only Google account it connects to is one I created and control for this work.

What Google data it accesses

When I authorize Lesson Draft Staging, Google shows exactly what it's allowed to access. It requests these permissions:

PermissionWhat it's used for
See, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app
drive.file		 Creating staging folders and Docs, uploading supporting files, and reading and updating those same files during review.
Associate you with your personal info on Google
openid		 Confirming which Google account is signed in.
See your primary Google Account email address
email		 Checking that the signed-in account is the staging account.

Within the files it created, the app reads file names, IDs, revision details, and the content and formatting of the Docs, and it downloads supporting files that have changed. It can't see other files in the account.

How that data is used

Lesson Draft Staging uses Google data only to create, read, update, and organize lesson drafts as part of my drafting and review workflow. It doesn't permanently delete anything in Google Drive. It isn't used for advertising or profiling, and it's never sold or shared with data brokers.

AI processing

Drafting is done with AI assistants. Draft text and file names from the staging account are sent to OpenAI (ChatGPT) and Anthropic (Claude) when those assistants review and revise a draft, and their services return the results to me. Nothing from the staging account goes to any other outside service. Google user data is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine learning models, and both AI services are set so this content isn't used to train their models.

Limited Use

Lesson Draft Staging's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Drafts live in the staging account's Google Drive. The workstation keeps the app's Google authorization, the staging account's email address, a record of the files it has staged, and review snapshots: copies of draft text, formatting, and changed supporting files taken at each review. The authorization is readable only by the background service that runs the app. The workstation is a dedicated computer used only for this work, protected by full-disk encryption, and no one else has access to it.

Keeping and deleting data

Drafts stay in the staging account until I move or delete them. Review snapshots stay on the workstation as a record of each revision until I delete them. If I stop using Lesson Draft Staging, I'll revoke its access and delete its stored authorization.

Revoking access

Anyone can see and remove the apps connected to their Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections. Removing Lesson Draft Staging there cuts off its access right away. You can also contact me to ask what data it holds and to have that data deleted.

Children

Neither the site nor the app is directed at children under 13, and neither knowingly collects their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
